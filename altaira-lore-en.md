# Altaira — Lore & Project Decisions

---

## The World

**Name:** Altaira

**Name origin:** The brightest star in Altaira's sky is called Altair by its inhabitants. Ancient belief holds that the world was forged by the light of that star — and so it bears its name.

**Tone:** Epic, mystical, with medieval fantasy elements. Heavily inspired by Lords of Magic Special Edition, with its own identity.

**Core elements:** Faiths, magic, stars, natural elements, racial diversity.

---

## The 8 Faiths

Each faith defines territory, playable races, available spells, and the player's AI Companion. Names are kept simple and universal by design.

| Faith | Suggested Color |

| Earth | Brown / Gold |

---

## Races

- Maximum of **3 playable races per faith**
- Each faith may have additional mythological creatures as NPCs/enemies
- Example (from the original Lords of Magic, names will be adapted):
  - **Life:** Elves, Hobbits, Dryads
  - **Death:** Dark Elves, Vampires, Shadows

> Renaming all races to avoid copyright is a future task.

---

## The Altaira Emblem

An 8-pointed star where each point represents a faith with its corresponding color. Maintains the visual reference to the star Altair and serves as the game's unifying symbol.

---

## AI Companion

- Every player receives a companion upon choosing their faith
- The companion is aligned to the chosen faith — personality, values, tone of voice, and worldview reflect that faith
- Companion functions:
  - **Living tutorial** — guides the player without a boring tutorial screen
  - **Ongoing guide** — suggests activities based on the player's situation
  - **Retention mechanic** — DMs the player to invite them back to play
  - **Emotional bond** — adventure partner, optional romantic interest
- Each companion has a unique personality, not a generic one

- **Bot name:** Altaira
- **Future integration:** NaviButler (existing modular bot engine)

## Inspiration & Copyright

- Heavily inspired by Lords of Magic Special Edition (Sierra, 1997)
- Original game names will be adapted — concepts and structure are copyright-free
- Positioning: "inspired by", not "based on"
- Faiths keep their original names (Life, Death, Fire, Water, Air, Earth, Order, Chaos) as they are universal concepts, not intellectual property
